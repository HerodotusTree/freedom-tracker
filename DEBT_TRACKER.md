Notes on the readings:

- **Series 1 (Treasury daily).** *Debt to the Penny* is published once per business day and reports the prior business day's close. Nov 5, 2026 is the latest print available at baseline time. Any "live" debt counter on the web is an interpolation between these daily prints, not an official real-time figure (see Gaps).
- **Series 2 (household).** The NY Fed's own page states the Q2 2026 total as $18.8 trillion and the quarterly change as −$13 billion (−0.1%). More precise decimals ($18.771 trillion) circulate in secondary summaries of the same report but were not verified from the NY Fed page fetched for this baseline, so the tracker's figure of record is the NY Fed's published $18.8 trillion. The NY Fed attributes most of the quarter's mortgage-balance decline (−$74 billion) to a mortgage servicer transfer gap in reporting, and states balances would otherwise have been flat.
- **Series 3 (net interest).** Two MTS readings are on record: FYTD through Aug 31, 2026 = $1,017.0 billion (MTS Table 9), and the closed fiscal year through Sep 30, 2026 = $1.052 trillion (September 2026 MTS). FY2026 is the first fiscal year in which net interest topped $1 trillion. Gross interest on Treasury securities for FY2026 was about $1.36 trillion (same MTS reporting); net interest is the figure used here because it is the cost borne to outside creditors.
- **Series 4 (global).** The IIF's September 23, 2026 edition is the latest *Global Debt Monitor* at baseline time; its headline figure is "surpassed $365 trillion in H1 2026." The prior edition (May 6, 2026, Q1 2026 data) put global debt at $352.7 trillion (IIF GDM Table 1, Global total), which supplies the previous period for the direction check in §2.
