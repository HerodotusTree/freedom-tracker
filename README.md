# The Freedom Tracker

**Damien Hall / Herodotus Tree**

Two files you give to your own AI, and a ledger that tells the truth.

**THE_FREEDOM_TRACKER_PROMPT** (PDF or Markdown — same words)
Upload it into a new conversation with your AI. It freezes the United States national debt at a baseline and tracks it one real print at a time — the Treasury publishes the number once per business day — on a ledger you hold and can check yourself. It is called the Freedom Tracker because debt and freedom are equal and opposites on the same scale: every dollar the debt falls, freedom rises by a dollar.

**STARTER_QUESTIONS** (PDF or Markdown)
Most people don't care about the national debt, and that's fine — they care about themselves. So start there: your debts, your interest per day, your portion of the whole. The country's numbers make sense once yours do. The file is alive: as people ask better first questions, it is updated and re-issued.

**DEBT_TRACKER.md**
The tracker's own book: the frozen baseline (November 5, 2026: $40,556,120,784,983.65, from the Treasury's Debt to the Penny) and the grading rule, fixed in advance — one print in a direction is an idea, two is a coincidence, three is a pattern. Prints move both ways inside a trend; the count never moves its goalposts.
